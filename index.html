Designing Sustainable Cities through Digital Twins

Planning and decision support for sustainable urban design using real-world data from the Bologna City Digital Twin.

Digital TwinsGISPlanningRoutingMachine Learning

Possible directions

  • Suggest optimal placement of green cells under cost, feasibility and accessibility constraints.
  • Optimise composite objectives such as coverage and sustainability.
  • Identify optimal routes for citizens using existing and planned infrastructure.
  • Study the robustness of optimisation models and planners under different types of noise.

LLMsPlanningReasoningEvaluation

Machine LearningDeep LearningAnomaly DetectionFairnessClinical Data

Possible directions

Cross-cutting research themes

Possible thesis angles across projects

Fairness & Bias

Fairness evaluation, bias detection and mitigation, intersectional analysis and distributional impacts.

Explainability & Human Oversight

Understand why AI systems fail or make recommendations and design mechanisms that support meaningful human control.

Study model and optimisation behaviour under noise, uncertainty, domain shift or changing environments.

Combine data-driven AI with explicit representations, reasoning, planning and constraints.
