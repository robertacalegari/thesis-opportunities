Thesis & Internship Opportunities
Available Topics

I supervise Master’s theses, project theses, and internships across Responsible AI, Trustworthy AI, human-centred systems, data-driven decision-making, reasoning, and their real-world applications.

These projects can be adapted to the student's background and interests.
Selected research area

Trustworthy, Fair & Responsible AI

Topics at the intersection of Trustworthy AI, fairness, bias mitigation, explainability and reliable decision-making.

Cross-cutting themes

Research dimensions

Fairness & Bias

Fairness evaluation, bias detection and mitigation, intersectional analysis and distributional impacts.

Explainability & Human Oversight

Understand why AI systems fail or make recommendations and design mechanisms that support meaningful human control.

Robustness & Reliability

Study models and decision systems under noise, uncertainty, domain shift or changing environments.

Symbolic & Neuro-Symbolic AI

Combine data-driven AI with explicit representations, reasoning, planning and constraints.
